Text Updates

Kinetic Family can send your family's schedule by text each morning — so you know what the day holds without opening anything. Texts are entirely optional. Here's exactly what we send, how often, and how to stop.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Texting is optional. Kinetic works fully without it, and consent to receive texts is never a condition of using the service or of any purchase.

Who Sends These Messages

Text messages come from Kinetic Family, a service of Kinetic Harmony LLC (Fort Lauderdale, Florida). Messages are sent from +1 (844) 991-3182 and may be delivered as SMS or as RCS, depending on your phone and carrier. The same opt-in, opt-out, and content rules apply either way.

How to Opt In

There is no way to receive texts from Kinetic without asking for them first. You opt in one of these ways:

  1. On the web. Enter your mobile number and check the consent box at app.kineticfam.com/text-me. The box is unchecked by default and the form will not submit without it. We then text you a welcome message confirming you're enrolled.
  2. In the Kinetic app. Add your mobile number under the Family tab and confirm it with the one-time code we text you. Once the number is verified, you can choose Text as your delivery method for any category of update under the Connect tab.
  3. By texting us first. If you start the conversation by texting +1 (844) 991-3182, we reply and confirm before enrolling you in the daily briefing.

Every path requires an action from you on your own phone — either checking the consent box, entering a code we texted you, or texting us first. We never add a number that arrived any other way.

The consent you agree to, word for word

"I agree to receive text messages from Kinetic Family at the number provided, including my daily morning briefing and replies to my texts. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase."

What We Send

  • Your morning briefing — the day's events, times, and locations, delivered first thing each morning at the time your family chooses
  • Schedule changes — when something you're tracking moves or is cancelled
  • Departure reminders — when it's time to leave, based on current traffic
  • Replies to your own texts — text us to add something to the calendar and we'll confirm it

We do not send marketing or promotional messages to this number, and we never text you about anything you haven't asked us to track.

We never sell your phone number, and we never share mobile opt-in information or consent with third parties for their own marketing. Your number is used to deliver the messages described on this page and nothing else.

Changing Your Mind

Replying STOP is immediate and permanent until you ask us to start again — there's no confirmation step to wade through and nobody will email you asking why. If you later want texts back, turn them on again from the Connect tab, or text us and we'll re-confirm.
